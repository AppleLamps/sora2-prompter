## Live Action Cinematic

### Dramatic Scene (Standard)
```
A rain-soaked city street at midnight, neon signs reflecting in puddles on the asphalt. A lone figure in a trench coat stands under a flickering streetlamp, steam rising from a manhole cover nearby. The air is thick with atmosphere as distant sirens echo through empty streets.

Cinematography:
Camera: medium close-up, low angle looking up
Mood: noir, mysterious, tense anticipation
Lighting: high contrast, cool blue streetlight with warm neon glow

Actions:
- The figure adjusts their collar, water dripping from the brim of their hat
- They glance down the street as car headlights sweep around the corner
- A taxi pulls up, its yellow cutting through the blue darkness

Dialogue:
- Figure: "You're late."
- Driver (O.S.): "Traffic was a nightmare."
```

### Romantic Comedy (Ultra-Detailed)
```
Style: Modern romantic comedy with warm, inviting aesthetic. Shot on ARRI Alexa with Cooke S4 lenses for gentle falloff and organic skin tones. Bright, airy lighting with soft golden hour feel throughout.

Format & Look
180° shutter; clean digital capture with subtle film emulation; warm color temperature; light film grain for texture; no gate weave.

Lenses & Filtration
35 mm and 50 mm Cooke S4 primes; Tiffen Black Pro-Mist 1/8 for gentle halation; slight diffusion for romantic softness.

Grade / Palette
Highlights: warm golden sunlight with subtle amber lift.
Mids: creamy skin tones with slight warmth in shadows.
Blacks: rich but not crushed, maintaining detail in darker areas.

Lighting & Atmosphere
Natural golden hour sunlight from camera right.
Soft bounce from 4×4 ultrabounce to fill shadows.
Practical: warm café lights strung overhead.
Atmos: gentle lens flare from setting sun, light dust motes in air.

Location & Framing
Charming European café terrace, golden hour.
Foreground: marble bistro table with two espresso cups.
Midground: couple leaning toward each other, laughing.
Background: cobblestone street, pedestrians in soft focus.

Wardrobe / Props / Extras
Main subjects: woman in flowing cream blouse, man in navy linen shirt.
Extras: background café patrons in muted earth tones.
Props: espresso cups, smartphone, leather journal.

Sound
Diegetic only: distant city hum, quiet café chatter, clinking porcelain, soft laughter.

Optimized Shot List
0.00–9.00 — "Connection" (35 mm, slow push-in)
Camera slowly pushes toward couple as they lean closer, golden hour light catching their hair. Background café patrons soften into warm bokeh. Purpose: build intimate connection.

9.00–15.00 — "The Moment" (50 mm, hold)
Tighter shot as they share a laugh, eyes meeting. Warm light flares gently across lens. Purpose: capture emotional peak with minimal movement.
```

Lighting: hard key light from window, warm fill from desk lamp
Mood: gritty, tense, melancholic

Actions:
- Detective takes a long drag from cigarette, exhales slowly
- He picks up a photo from desk, studies it intently
- Phone rings, he hesitates before answering

Dialogue:
- Detective: "Yeah, I'm still working on it."
- Voice on phone: "They're getting impatient, Frank."
